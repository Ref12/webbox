Rules

Place exactly one marker in every row, column and colour region. Markers may not touch, not even diagonally. Markers far apart on a diagonal are fine.

Tap a cell: empty → ✕ → marker → empty. Long-press and drag to paint ✕s (or switch on Drag ✕). Keys: arrows move, space/x cycle, u undo, h hint.

Colour challenge swaps the palette for near shades of one hue; each region gets a glyph instead.
